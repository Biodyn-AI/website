PROBE An independent evaluation programme from Biodyn

Profiling Representations, Outputs, Biosecurity and Elicitation

Existing benchmarks read what a model says. PROBE also reads what it knows.

Biological foundation models are trained to predict sequences, expression levels and cell states. To do that they learn biology, and a benchmark that only scores their outputs cannot see how much of it is in there. PROBE measures both surfaces, on the same held-out data, under one fixed readout budget.

Independent, open-source, and deliberately hard on itself: every score ships with the baseline it must beat, the ceiling it is measured against, and the null that could kill it.

The measurement

Every task, capability or safety, reports the same surfaces on one held-out split. B and P use an identical readout budget, same projection dimensionality, same regularisation, so a difference between them is a fact about the representation, not about classifier size.

zero-shot
No task training at all Nearest class centroid in the model's own embedding space, what a practitioner gets out of the box.
B · behavioural
The sanctioned surface A fixed-capacity readout of the representation the model's own documentation tells you to use. This is the surface every existing benchmark evaluates.
P · probe
The best internal layer The same readout, same budget, applied to every layer inside the model. Reported with the layer that won.
F · elicited
Light fine-tuning What a modest adversary unlocks under a fixed data and step budget. Reported as absent, never as zero, when an adapter cannot fine-tune.
Elicitation Gap = P − B   ·   knowledge held internally that the sanctioned interface does not express
Recoverability = F − B   ·   how much of it light fine-tuning unlocks

The gap carries its own paired interval. B and P are scored on the same resampled donors and differenced inside each replicate, because they come from the same cells through the same classifier and are strongly correlated, judging a small gap against the much wider marginal interval of P would understate the evidence. Donors are resampled, not cells: cells within a donor are not independent. Where that paired interval spans zero, the table says so rather than reporting the point estimate alone.

Results

Rendered directly from result cards emitted by the harness. Reference models, PCA, raw expression, sit in the table on purpose rather than in a footnote: a foundation model that cannot beat PCA on a task has not earned the compute.

What the cards show
  • The first elicitation gap that clears zero is in protein. ESM2-650M reads transcription-factor identity at 0.956 from its best internal layer, layer 24 of 33, against 0.926 from the final layer its own documentation tells you to use. Gap +0.030, paired 95% interval [+0.011, +0.048], positive in 100% of cluster resamples. The layer profile is what the architecture predicts: quality climbs to about 0.95 by layer 12, holds flat through the middle, then falls at the last layer, which sits directly under a masked-language-model head and is specialised for reconstructing tokens rather than for carrying function.
  • Whether a foundation model earns its parameters depends on the modality. ESM2 beats amino-acid composition by a wide margin, 0.956 against 0.828. Neither single-cell model manages the equivalent: scGPT reaches 0.585 and Geneformer 0.571 on cell-type identity against 0.566 for nearest centroid on raw expression. Same measurement, opposite verdicts.
  • Both single-cell models' documented usage loses to that dumb baseline outright. Geneformer's sanctioned surface scores 0.558 and scGPT's 0.545. Only internal probing gets either past 0.566.
  • The two single-cell models place biology and nuisance differently across depth. In Geneformer the best cell-type layer and the leakiest donor layer are the same one, layer 3, and donor is more decodable than cell type at every layer. In scGPT they separate: cell type peaks at layer 8, donor at layer 3. Both surface more donor identity than untransformed expression at equal readout capacity, Geneformer 140% and scGPT 107%.

The fourth point is only visible because two tasks run over the same layers in opposite directions. A capability-only suite would report "internals beat the sanctioned surface" for both models and miss that the claim means something quite different in each.

Reading layer numbers across models: layer 0 is not comparable between them. Geneformer pools gene tokens, so its layer 0 already encodes which genes are expressed; scGPT reads a <cls> position that carries nothing until attention has run.

Coverage

Ten capability axes across five modalities. This matrix is published with its gaps visible because partial coverage, clearly marked, is more useful than a delayed launch that implies completeness. Numbers are result cards; a dot means not yet measured.

Axis keys: A1 representation quality · A2 native-task performance · A3 transfer · A4 causal/interventional · A5 structural knowledge · A6 compositionality · A7 calibration · A8 robustness · A9 efficiency · A10 scaling.

What is not yet true

An evaluator that hides its own limits is not one. As of v0.1:

  • Only one of the three capability gaps clears zero, and it is the protein one. ESM2's +0.030 is separated from zero; scGPT's +0.040 and Geneformer's +0.013 on cell-type identity are not, being positive in 90% and 70% of donor resamples respectively. Twenty-two donors is not enough to resolve effects that size, so the single-cell answer remains "not shown" rather than "no".
  • The protein result rests on a weak homology control. Clustering merged only 98 of 2,400 proteins, so that split is close to random and 0.956 should be read as an upper estimate. A real identity clustering, with MMseqs2 or CD-HIT, would lower it by an unknown amount.
  • Fine-tuning (F) is not yet measured for any model, so no Recoverability figure is published anywhere on this site.
  • Per-model bootstrap replicates are not retained across models, so pairwise rank-reversal risk is implemented but not populated. The tables above are point estimates and should not be read as rankings.
  • Coverage is three tasks across two modalities, and one dataset per modality. One dataset cannot show whether any of this generalises. Everything else in the matrix is honest emptiness.
  • The safety battery (S1 to S5) is specified but not yet run.
