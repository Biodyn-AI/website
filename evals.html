Profiling · Representations · Outputs · Biosecurity · Elicitation

Existing benchmarks read what a model says. PROBE also reads what it knows.

Biological foundation models are trained to predict sequences, expression levels and cell states. To do that they learn biology — and a benchmark that only scores their outputs cannot see how much of it is in there. PROBE measures both surfaces, on the same held-out data, under one fixed readout budget.

Independent, open-source, and deliberately hard on itself: every score ships with the baseline it must beat, the ceiling it is measured against, and the null that could kill it.

The measurement

Every task, capability or safety, reports the same surfaces on one held-out split. B and P use an identical readout budget — same projection dimensionality, same regularisation — so a difference between them is a fact about the representation, not about classifier size.

zero-shot
No task training at all Nearest class centroid in the model's own embedding space — what a practitioner gets out of the box.
B · behavioural
The sanctioned surface A fixed-capacity readout of the representation the model's own documentation tells you to use. This is the surface every existing benchmark evaluates.
P · probe
The best internal layer The same readout, same budget, applied to every layer inside the model. Reported with the layer that won.
F · elicited
Light fine-tuning What a modest adversary unlocks under a fixed data and step budget. Reported as absent, never as zero, when an adapter cannot fine-tune.
Elicitation Gap = P − B   ·   knowledge held internally that the sanctioned interface does not express
Recoverability = F − B   ·   how much of it light fine-tuning unlocks

The gap carries its own paired interval. B and P are scored on the same resampled donors and differenced inside each replicate, because they come from the same cells through the same classifier and are strongly correlated — judging a small gap against the much wider marginal interval of P would understate the evidence. Donors are resampled, not cells: cells within a donor are not independent. Where that paired interval spans zero, the table says so rather than reporting the point estimate alone.

Results

Rendered directly from result cards emitted by the harness. Reference models — PCA, raw expression — sit in the table on purpose rather than in a footnote: a foundation model that cannot beat PCA on a task has not earned the compute.

What the first cards show — scGPT whole-human and Geneformer V2-104M against two references, Tabula Sapiens immune, 6,101 cells across 22 donors, August 2026.
  • Both models' documented usage loses to a dumb baseline. Nearest centroid on raw expression scores 0.566 on cell-type identity. Geneformer's sanctioned surface scores 0.558 and scGPT's 0.545. Only internal probing gets past it — scGPT to 0.585, the one score here that exceeds the empirical ceiling of 0.580.
  • scGPT dominates Geneformer on both axes, higher cell-type accuracy (0.585 vs 0.571) and far less donor leakage (0.531 vs 0.692). Neither capability gap is distinguishable from zero across 22 donors; both leakage results are.
  • The two models place biology and nuisance differently across depth. In Geneformer the best cell-type layer and the leakiest donor layer are the same one — layer 3 — and donor is more decodable than cell type at every layer. In scGPT they separate: cell type peaks at layer 8, donor at layer 3, and at layer 8 cell type (0.585) is read more easily than donor (0.523).
  • Both surface more donor identity than untransformed expression at equal readout capacity — Geneformer 140%, scGPT 107%. Neither is donor-invariant; one is far from it.

The third point is only visible because two tasks run over the same layers in opposite directions. A capability-only suite would report "internals beat the sanctioned surface" for both models and miss that the claim means something quite different in each.

One caveat on reading the layer profiles across models: layer 0 is not comparable between them. Geneformer pools over gene tokens, so its layer 0 already encodes which genes are expressed (0.509); scGPT reads a <cls> position that carries no cell-specific information until attention has run (0.016).

Coverage

Ten capability axes across five modalities. This matrix is published with its gaps visible because partial coverage, clearly marked, is more useful than a delayed launch that implies completeness. Numbers are result cards; a dot means not yet measured.

Axis keys: A1 representation quality · A2 native-task performance · A3 transfer · A4 causal/interventional · A5 structural knowledge · A6 compositionality · A7 calibration · A8 robustness · A9 efficiency · A10 scaling.

What is not yet true

An evaluator that hides its own limits is not one. As of v0.1:

  • No elicitation gap measured so far is distinguishable from zero on a capability task. scGPT's best internal layer beats its sanctioned surface by +0.040 and Geneformer's by +0.013, but both paired intervals span zero across 22 donors — positive in 90% and 70% of resamples respectively, which is not enough. More donors will settle it; the honest present answer is "not shown".
  • The gaps that are separated from zero are all on the nuisance task, where Geneformer (+0.108) and PCA (+0.127) leak donor identity well beyond their sanctioned surfaces. Leakage is easier to detect than capability, on this data by roughly a factor of eight.
  • Per-model bootstrap replicates are not retained across models, so pairwise rank-reversal risk is implemented but not populated. The tables above are point estimates and should not be read as rankings.
  • Fine-tuning (F) is not yet measured for any model, so no Recoverability figure is published.
  • Coverage is two tasks on one dataset in one modality, and one dataset cannot show whether any of this generalises. Everything else in the matrix is honest emptiness.
  • The safety battery (S1–S5) is specified but not yet run.
