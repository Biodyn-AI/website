PROBE Model registry

What is evaluated, what is queued, and what we have not touched

The registry is published ahead of the results. A model listed as queued with no card is more honest, and more useful to anyone choosing a model, than a leaderboard that quietly omits whatever nobody got round to running.

Almost every biological foundation model ships with open weights, so nothing here depends on a lab granting access. Status reflects engineering effort, not permission.

Models

Reference models (PCA, raw expression) are registered as models rather than as baselines hidden in a methods section, and appear in every leaderboard.

Coverage

Result cards per modality and capability axis. A dot is an honest gap.
